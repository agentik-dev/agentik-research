# agentik-research

The public research site on long-running Claude Code sessions and the agentik tools. Work arrives as packets from the suite's management repo.

- Write only in this repo. `.sources/*` and any attached agentik-* checkout are read-only sources, and their `CLAUDE.md` files do not bind you here.
- Name nothing private: no private repos, hosts, domains, addresses, paths, devices, households, or people other than published authors.
- Cite only what you opened in this session. Footnotes go under `## Sources`, and a source note goes in `research/sources/`.
- `make check` passes before every push. Never push to `main`, never force-push.
- Prose in `docs/` follows respeak, below. Drafts go in `research/synthesis/`.
