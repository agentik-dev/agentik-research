# OUTLINE: the site, page by page

Phase 3 draft, 2026-10-14. Each page lists its claims in order with `[slug; level]`, where the slug is a note in `research/sources/` and the level is measured, documented, reported, our use or conjecture. Volatile facts are dated "as of October 2026". Pages in bluf mode (index, summary) lead with the result; the rest are technical mode for a peer engineer. ## docs/index.md (bluf, exec)

1. What this is: research on what goes wrong when Claude Code sessions run for hours or unattended, what helps, and why six small tools exist. One owner, young tools, every claim cited and levelled.
2. Five findings:
   - Length alone lowers accuracy, even with perfect retrieval: 0.92 to 0.68 at 3,000 tokens; Claude 3.5 Sonnet 87.5 to 29.8 percent at 32K on low-overlap retrieval; Claude Opus 4 44.6 percent at 500 instructions [levy-same-task-more-tokens; modarressi-nolima; du-context-length-alone; jaroslawicz-ifscale; measured]. Anthropic documents "context rot" [anthropic-context-windows-doc; documented].
   - Compaction keeps the gist and loses the specifics (artifact trail 2.45 of 5; 3 of 3 obscure values lost); no study compares it with a restart from a handoff; Anthropic's own harness work carried state in files and git [factory-evaluating-compression; anthropic-cookbook-context-engineering; measured] [anthropic-engineering-effective-harnesses-compaction; anthropic-engineering-harness-design; reported].
   - Many agents help decomposable work (+80.8 percent) and hurt sequential work (-39 to -70 percent), at 3 to 10 times the tokens [kim-scaling-agent-systems; measured] [claude-blog-building-multi-agent-systems; documented].
   - Review without an outside signal makes answers worse (95.5 to 89.0 percent over two rounds); returns per round fall fast (+5.8, +2.4, +0.4, +0.6); Claude Code caps a Stop-hook loop at 8 [huang-self-correct; wang-swe-review; measured] [code-docs-hooks-reference; documented].
   - An unattended run's "success" is unproven until you read `permission_denials`, cap money twice (the cap overshoots), and watch progress from outside (agents never notice their first error in 38.5 percent of cases) [code-docs-headless-unattended; code-docs-agent-sdk-agent-loop-unattended; documented] [arxiv-locating-hidden-failures; measured].
3. What the tools are (one line each) and what this site does not claim: no measurement shows the tools beat the alternatives; their evidence is one owner's record [agentik-session-readme; agentik-relay-readme; our use].
4. Where to start: practices for the engineer; tools/index for the evaluator; field for the evidence; method for the limits.
