## What it says
- The abstract names three ways to cut inference cost: prompt adaptation, LLM approximation and LLM cascade; FrugalGPT is a cascade that learns which model combination to use per query. It reports matching GPT-4 with up to 98 percent cost reduction, or improving accuracy over GPT-4 by 4 percent at the same cost. It notes API prices differ by up to two orders of magnitude.

## Limits
- I read only the abstract page. Models and prices are those of 2023; the figures are the authors' on their selected datasets, and a 2026 benchmark (li-llmrouterbench) found binary routers of this family struggled to save cost without losing accuracy.
