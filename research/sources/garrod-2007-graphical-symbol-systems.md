## What it says
- The authors test whether iconic drawings become symbolic through repeated use, with an interactive drawing-and-guessing task across three experiments.
- Experiment 1: repetition alone is not enough; feedback and interaction between the two communicators are needed. Experiment 2: the amount of interaction changes how the signs evolve, through a process of grounding.
- Experiment 3: people who were not part of the interaction have difficulty interpreting the evolved signs. The authors conclude that meaning moves from the sign itself to the users' memory of how it was used.

## Limits
- I read the abstract record from the Europe PMC search service, not the paper.
- Drawing pairs in a laboratory, not agents or engineers. The claim carried over to agent shorthand and a supervising person is an analogy, not a finding.
