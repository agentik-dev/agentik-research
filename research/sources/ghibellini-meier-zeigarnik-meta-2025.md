## What it says
I opened the RePEc abstract page; the publisher's page redirected to a login step.
- Zeigarnik effect (better memory for unfinished than finished tasks): the meta-analysis found no memory advantage for unfinished tasks. The authors call it hard to replicate and name situational factors that may matter, such as the experimenter's authority, the demands of the task and how involved participants were.
- Ovsiankina effect (a tendency to resume an interrupted task): found as a general tendency.

## Limits
- Abstract only; it gives no study count and no effect sizes. The memory advantage is not the effect that Masicampo and Baumeister measured (intrusive thoughts and interference from an unfinished goal, which a plan removes), so this is context for the field, not a failed replication of their plan-making result.
