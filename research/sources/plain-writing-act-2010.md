## What it says
- Enacted 13 October 2010 as Public Law 111-274.
- Section 3 defines plain writing as writing that is clear, concise and well-organized, and that follows other best practices suited to the subject, the field and the intended audience. The statute sets no numeric target for sentence length or reading level.
- Section 4 duties, within 9 months of enactment: each agency head designates senior officials, tells employees about the Act, trains them in plain writing, sets up a compliance process, keeps a plain writing section on the agency website, and names points of contact for public input. Within 1 year of enactment each agency must use plain writing in every covered document it issues or substantially revises.
- The Director of the Office of Management and Budget had 6 months to issue guidance. Until then agencies could follow the writing guidelines of the Plain Language Action and Information Network, or their own consistent guidance.

## Limits
- It binds United States federal agencies and their public-facing documents. It is a legal duty, not evidence that plain writing works.
