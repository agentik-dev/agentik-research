## What it says
- MADR, the Markdown Architectural Decision Records template, has existed since 2017. Version 3.0 added optional metadata and an optional confirmation section and merged the positive and negative consequences sections; version 4.0 renamed some elements and introduced a minimal variant (as of the page's April 2026 update).
- The full template follows a decision from identification to enforcement: title, metadata (status, date, deciders, consulted, informed, all optional), context and problem statement, decision drivers, considered options, decision outcome with consequences, an optional confirmation (how the decision is checked, for instance by review or test), pros and cons of each option, and more information.
- The minimal variant keeps three to five core elements, which the page says is close to Nygard's 2011 proposal and to Y-statements.
- Status values listed: proposed, rejected, accepted, deprecated, and superseded by (others possible).
- The page gives no guidance on length and no supersession process beyond the status value.

## Limits
- A primer by the template's authors, not an evaluation; no data on which variant teams keep up to date.
