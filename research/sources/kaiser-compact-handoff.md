## What it says
- A three-question rule. Do you still need the whole conversation? If yes, rewind out failed detours and continue. What can you afford to lose? Decisions kept and specifics lost: compact; facts kept in readable form but momentum lost: handoff; only the answer: subagent. When are you coming back? Later today: compact; tomorrow or later: handoff.
- Says compaction keeps decisions and overall shape and loses specifics. Because auto-compact runs near a full window, when the summary is built from a degraded session, she compacts manually at around 50% of the window.
- A handoff is about five lines: starting point, decisions made, open items, key file names, the next step. She has the session write it and checks what is missing before closing. A handoff loses the background behind the decisions, the reasoning and the working atmosphere of a live session, so she keeps project notes in files as well.

## Limits
- Personal practice, with one handoff that failed and was fixed by returning to the old session. No measurements; the 50% figure and the cache-timing claims are rules of thumb.
