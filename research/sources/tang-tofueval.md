## What it says
- A benchmark of topic-focused dialogue summaries written by language models of different sizes, with binary sentence-level human labels for factual consistency and explanations for the inconsistent sentences.
- Language models produce "significant amounts of factual errors in the dialogue domain", whatever their size, across a range of error types.
- Language models used as binary factual-consistency judges, GPT-4 included, performed poorly and could be beaten by specialized factuality metrics.

## Limits
- Abstract page only; it gives no error rates or scores.
- Dialogue summaries from 2024-era models. It supports caution about model-written summaries and about using a model to check another model's summary, not any particular rate.
