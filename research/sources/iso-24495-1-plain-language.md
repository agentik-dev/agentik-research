## What it says
- The entry lists a publication date of 1 June 2023 and a download file dated 25 November 2024.
- The standard "establishes governing principles and guidelines for developing plain language documents". It is useful to anyone who writes or helps write documents; its widest use is for documents aimed at the general public, and it also applies to technical writing, legislative drafting and controlled languages.
- It applies to most written languages but gives examples only in English, and covers only printed or digital information that is mainly text. It leaves out existing technical guidance on accessibility and digital documents.
- Two more parts are planned: Part 2 on legal communications and Part 3 on science writing.
