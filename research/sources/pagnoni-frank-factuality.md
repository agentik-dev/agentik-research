## What it says
- The authors say modern summarizers produce "highly fluent but often factually unreliable outputs", and that factuality metrics could not be compared because there was no common benchmark.
- They built a typology of factual errors and collected human annotations of summaries from state-of-the-art systems on two news datasets (CNN/DM and XSum), then checked automatic factuality metrics against the human judgments.

## Limits
- Abstract page only: no error rates or correlations, and the typology's categories are not listed on the page.
- 2021 systems and news summarization.
