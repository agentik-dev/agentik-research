## What it says
- States that the list "began as a National Patient Safety Goal in 2004" and has since become a standing accreditation expectation rather than a standalone goal.
- Groups the required entries as six: U (unit), IU, daily and every-other-day forms, trailing and leading zero, MS, and MSO4 and MgSO4. This is the same content as the eight rows in the reproduction I opened (joint-commission-do-not-use-list-utmb-copy), counted differently.
- Gives a worked example for "U": a handwritten "4U" misread as "40" units, a tenfold overdose. The page cites no source for the example.
- Says each entry is banned for a documented, recurring pattern of misreading handwritten or hurried text.

## Limits
- A guide site, not the accreditor; no sources are cited for the example, so treat the "4U" case as an illustration, not a counted incident.
- Written in 2026, so it describes the list now; the 2004 start is repeated from other sources.
