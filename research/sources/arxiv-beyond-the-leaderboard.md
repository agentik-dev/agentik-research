## What it says
- A literature synthesis of 27 benchmark, taxonomy and audit papers published between 2023 and 2026, covering 19 benchmarks, grouped into six failure clusters: tool invocation and parameters; planning and constraint satisfaction; degradation over long horizons from accumulating context; multi-agent coordination; safety and security under adversarial or underspecified conditions; and measurement validity.
- Across that literature the authors find that failures compound non-linearly as tasks get longer, that strong subtask results do not reliably give end-to-end success, and that added scaffolding does not consistently improve reliability. They note real progress in single-turn tool use, short-horizon web navigation and narrowly scoped coding.

## Limits
- Abstract only; I did not open the full paper. A review of other studies, so it adds no new measurement; the clustering is the authors' own.
