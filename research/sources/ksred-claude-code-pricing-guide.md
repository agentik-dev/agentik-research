## What it says
- The author's own logged Claude Code use against the Max plan he paid for: June 2025 421M tokens (API-equivalent about 897 US dollars), July 2025 2.4B tokens (about 5,623 dollars), August 2025 320M tokens (about 771 dollars), roughly 10B tokens in all (over 15,000 dollars API-equivalent) against about 800 dollars paid on Max across those periods (June 2025 to February 2026). Only June to August come from token-level data; later months are estimated from prompt counts and session logs.
- More than 90 percent of tokens were cache reads; the author prices 4.5B cache-read tokens at about 2,250 dollars against about 22,500 dollars at the full input rate. About 95 percent of the heavy period was Opus.
- Break-even claim (author's judgment): if the monthly API equivalent is over 100 dollars, Max 5x saves money, and Max 20x is cheaper above 200 dollars. Anthropic does not publish a token allowance for Pro or either Max tier, so limits cannot be known in advance.
- The author built a log-parsing tool (cctrack) and a hosted team product, so he has a commercial interest.

## Limits
- One heavy Opus user; the 15,000 dollars is API-equivalent value, not money spent; prices and plan terms in the page may not match October 2026. Reported evidence.
