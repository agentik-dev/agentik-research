## What it says
- A reference list of abbreviations, symbols and dose designations compiled from reports to ISMP's national medication-error reporting program that "have been misinterpreted and involved in harmful or potentially harmful medication errors."
- It advises against using them in any medication communication: spoken, electronic or handwritten, in prescriptions and on labels.
- Entries that also appear on The Joint Commission's "Do Not Use" list (standard IM.02.02.01) carry a double asterisk, and accredited organizations must add those to their own lists. Entries marked with a dagger apply mainly to handwritten communication.

## Limits
- I read the list's introduction; the page does not give error counts or explain the mechanism of each misreading. The Joint Commission's own list returned HTTP 403, so I rely on this page for its existence and rule.
- Medicine, handwritten orders and many hands; the carry-over to agent shorthand is an analogy.
