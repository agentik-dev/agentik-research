## What it says
- Defines an architectural decision record as a record of one architectural decision with its rationale, trade-offs and consequences, and the set of records kept in a project as its decision log.
- Credits Nygard's 2011 post with popularising the idea, names Y-statements as a compact format from Zdun and colleagues' work, and points to a comparison of seven templates as further reading.
- The front page does not state rules for size, lifecycle, status or superseding, and does not name adr-tools or log4brains in the text I read; those are on linked pages I did not open.

## Limits
- A directory page: it points to templates and tools rather than saying how long a record should be or how many to keep. I read the front page only.
