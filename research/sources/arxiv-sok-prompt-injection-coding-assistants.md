## What it says
- A systematization of knowledge (a literature review, not a new experiment): 78 studies from 2021 to 2026 synthesized, 42 attack techniques catalogued, 18 defence mechanisms reviewed.
- Reports attack success above 85% against state-of-the-art defences when attackers adapt, and that most of the 18 defences mitigate less than 50% of sophisticated adaptive attacks.
- Argues prompt injection should be treated as a first-class vulnerability needing architectural mitigations, not ad-hoc filtering.

## Limits
- A secondary synthesis; the abstract does not break the figures down by defence type, so it does not say how permission prompts, hooks or sandboxes fared. The "measured" label rests on the studies it summarizes, which were not individually read.
