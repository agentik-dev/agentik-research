## What it says
- Chain of Draft asks the model for brief intermediate reasoning steps that keep only essential information, in place of verbose step-by-step reasoning.
- It uses "as little as only 7.6%" of the tokens of chain-of-thought prompting, and the abstract says it matches or surpasses chain-of-thought accuracy on various reasoning tasks.

## Limits
- Abstract page only; no per-task accuracy or token figures, and the tasks are not named there. It concerns a model's own reasoning, not text a person must read.
