## What it says
- Plan prices as listed, as of October 2026, in US dollars: Free 0 (no Claude Code); Pro 20 per month, or 17 per month billed as 200 up front; Max from 100 per month, with 5x and 20x tiers; Team Standard seat 25 per month (20 annual); Team Premium seat 125 per month (100 annual); Enterprise 20 per seat per month on an annual term plus usage at API rates. All paid plans include Claude Code.
- Limits: every plan has usage limits that reset on a rolling five-hour window and paid plans also have weekly limits; Pro gives at least 5 times Free per session, Max gives 5 or 20 times Pro per session, Team Premium seats give 5 times Standard seats. Anthropic may add weekly or monthly caps or limit specific models. Claude on the web, desktop, mobile and Claude Code draw from the same pool.
- Extra usage: Pro and Max can turn on usage credits to keep working at standard API rates after hitting a limit; Team plans include extra usage; Enterprise bills usage at API rates.
- Model access: Pro, Max and Team include Opus, Sonnet and Haiku; Fable is through usage credits on Pro and limited to 50 percent of weekly limits on Max and on Team Premium seats; Enterprise lists Fable with no cap.
- Monthly API credits: Max 5x 100 and Max 20x 200 dollars; Team up to 500 dollars pooled (see anthropic-api-credits-subscribers for what they cover).
- Re-opened 2026-10-14 (site sitting): confirmed. FAQ "What are the usage limits on each plan, and what happens when I hit them?": every plan has limits that reset on a rolling five-hour session window and paid plans add weekly limits; activity across Claude on web, desktop, mobile and Claude Code draws from the same pool; the page says there is no fixed message count, since usage depends on conversation length, model and features. Pro gives at least 5 times Free per session and Max 5 or 20 times Pro per 5-hour session; Anthropic may add weekly or monthly caps. No token counts or message counts appear for Pro or Max.
