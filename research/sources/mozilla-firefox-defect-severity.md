## What it says
- The severity field shows the scope of a bug's effect on Firefox and is shown beside priority. New bugs start at `--`.
- Examples by level: S1 is WebExtensions disabled for all users, web search from the address bar not working, and crashes with data loss. S2 is a bug that could reasonably make a user switch browsers, through severity or frequency, plus fuzzing blockers and contractual-partner bugs that are not S1. S3 is routine items such as visual artifacts found during refactoring and QA reports with uncommon or hard-to-reproduce steps.
- Rule of thumb: most task and enhancement bugs take severity N/A; a defect never does.
- The same page points to a wiki page for the full list of values, which this session could not open.

## Limits
- Gives examples, not definitions, for S1 to S3, and does not mention S4. No rates or agreement figures.
