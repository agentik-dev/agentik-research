## What it says
A secondary news report; I did not open the primary paper (the publisher page redirected to a login step, and a project poster held no readable text).
- It summarises programmer-interruption research that the article attributes to Chris Parnin: 10,000 recorded programming sessions from 86 programmers using two development environments.
- Programmers took 10 to 15 minutes to resume editing code after an interruption; when interrupted in the middle of editing a method, they resumed within a minute only 10 percent of the time; a programmer was likely to get only one uninterrupted 2-hour session a day.

## Limits
- Secondary source for numbers whose primary paper I could not read. The article's framing may differ from the paper's. Research reported in 2013, before coding agents; an agent's question is a different kind of interruption from an editing-time one.
