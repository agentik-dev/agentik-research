## What it says
- Barbara Minto is named as the creator of the Pyramid Principle. The site describes the principle as organizing ideas "as a pyramid under a single point".
- The book *The Minto Pyramid Principle: Logic in Writing, Thinking and Problem Solving* (1996 edition) replaces the original *Pyramid Principle*, which the site says was first published in 1987 and reissued unchanged in 2002.
- Courses listed as of October 2026: a 2-day live course taught by Minto, covering the pyramid rules and her SCQ framework for identifying the reader's question; a self-study set (a 35-minute DVD overview, the textbook and a 192-page workbook); an online course of five interactive parts taking about 12 to 16 hours, priced at 1,200 USD for six months of access.
- The page does not itself spell out the answer-first, group-and-summarize steps.

## Limits
- The author's own sales page. It reports no test of whether the method improves reader action.
- Other notes in this repo give different first-publication years for the book; this page states 1987. I did not find a primary source that settles the other years.
