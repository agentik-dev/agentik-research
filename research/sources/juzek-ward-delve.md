## What it says
- The authors built a transferable method to find words whose frequency in scientific abstracts rose because of language-model use, and identified 21 such focal words.
- They looked for the cause of the over-use in model architecture, algorithm choices and training data and found no support; model testing is consistent with reinforcement learning from human feedback playing a role.
- An exploratory online study suggests participants may react differently to "delve" than to the other focal words.

## Limits
- Abstract page only; no effect sizes or participant counts.
- Another note in this repo records this paper as an ACL 2025 Findings paper; the page I read says COLING 2025. Check the venue before citing.
