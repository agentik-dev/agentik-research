## What it says
- A coarse-to-fine prompt-compression method with a budget controller, a token-level iterative compression algorithm and an instruction-tuning step to align the language models involved.
- Claims up to 20 times compression "with little performance loss", tested on GSM8K, BBH, ShareGPT and Arxiv-March23.

## Limits
- Abstract page only; it gives no exact figure for the performance loss and does not discuss whether a person can read the compressed prompt. The compression is for a model reader, which is the machine-lane case.
