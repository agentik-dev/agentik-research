## What it says
- Models that reason in human language give a "unique opportunity for AI safety", because their reasoning can be monitored for intent to misbehave.
- The authors say chain-of-thought monitoring is imperfect and can miss some misbehaviour, but recommend investing in it alongside other safety methods.
- They say monitorability may be fragile and that developers should consider how development decisions affect it. The abstract names no specific mechanism by which legibility would be lost.

## Limits
- A position paper by 41 authors; I read the abstract page only. It concerns a model's internal reasoning text, not messages between agents or shorthand in a status report.
- It supports the general point that oversight leans on language a person can read. It is not evidence about agent dialects.
