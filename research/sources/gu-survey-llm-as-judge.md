## What it says
Read from the abstract page only.
- The abstract says that making LLM-as-a-judge systems reliable "remains a significant challenge that requires careful design and standardization", and organises the survey around how to build reliable ones.
- It names three ways to improve reliability: improving consistency, mitigating biases, and adapting to different assessment settings. It also proposes ways to evaluate a judge's reliability with a new benchmark.

## Limits
- A survey whose abstract gives no figures; I did not read the body. It supports "judges need calibration", not any particular rate.
