## What it says
- The report recalculates three readability formulas, the Automated Readability Index, the Fog Count and the Flesch Reading Ease formula, for use with Navy material.
- Sample: 531 Navy enlisted personnel in four technical training schools at two bases (Naval Air Station Memphis and Great Lakes Navy Training Center).
- Passages: 18 passages from Navy Rate Training Manuals, which are technical training texts for adults.
- Criterion: each person's reading comprehension level (the comprehension section of a standard reading test) and comprehension of the 18 passages. The formulas were fitted to that criterion.

## Limits
- I read the repository's abstract page only. The PDF returned HTTP 403, so I did not read the formula coefficients or the fit statistics, and the abstract page does not name a "Flesch-Kincaid grade level".
- Fitted on one population (Navy trainees) and 18 passages, in 1975. The report does not test whether rewriting to a lower score raises comprehension.
