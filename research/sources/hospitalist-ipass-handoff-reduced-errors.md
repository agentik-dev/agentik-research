## What it says
- A news summary of the I-PASS handoff study in the New England Journal of Medicine; the page's own citation gives 2014.
- Sites: nine pediatric residency training programs in the United States and Canada, with 36 to 182 residents each.
- Numbers it repeats from the study: 10,740 patient admissions; medical errors from 24.5 to 18.8 per 100 admissions (23 percent fewer); preventable adverse events from 4.7 to 3.3 per 100 admissions (30 percent fewer). They match the abstract in starmer-ipass-handoff-nejm-2014.md.
- It spells out the mnemonic as illness severity; patient summary; action list; situation awareness and contingency plans; and synthesis by receiver.

## Limits
- A secondary source. Used here only for the pediatric setting and the program sizes, which the abstract does not give.
