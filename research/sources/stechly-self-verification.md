## What it says
Read from the abstract page only.
- The paper challenges the belief that a model can critique and improve its own solutions, which rests on the assumption that verifying is easier than generating.
- Tested with GPT-4 on Game of 24, graph colouring and STRIPS planning, with the model critiquing itself and with an external sound reasoner verifying.
- Reported results: a "significant performance collapse with self-critique" and "significant performance gains with sound external verification". Merely re-prompting with a sound verifier keeps most of the benefit of more involved setups.
