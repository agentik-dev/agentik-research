## What it says
- Corpus: more than 37,000 ACL Anthology papers from 2020 to 2024, plus 3,000 human-written passages each paired with a language-model "improved" version.
- Language-model-modified text more often contains certain syntactic constructions, uses longer and more complex words, and has lower lexical diversity.
- A pilot reading study with 20 domain experts found they rated the language-model-improved texts as more understandable and more exciting than the originals, while their written comments about language models were negative.

## Limits
- Abstract page only, with no effect sizes or rating scores. Twenty readers; scientific writing.
- It is the closest measured result I found to the question whether readers dislike machine-style text, and it points two ways: ratings of the text were favourable, comments about the technology were not.
