## What it says
- A self-contained benchmark that mimics a small software company (internal websites, data, coworkers to message). Agents browse, write code, run programs and communicate. The abstract says the most competitive agent could complete 30 percent of tasks autonomously, that simpler tasks were often solved autonomously, and that longer-horizon tasks remained beyond current systems.

## Limits
- Abstract page only; the full text, with cost, step counts and the authors' failure analysis, was not opened. The 30 percent figure is for the models and scaffold of the 2024 to 2025 versions, not for current models.
